The 53rd series of IANA's DNS Root Zone KSK ceremonies took place over two consecutive days at the ICANN Root Zone Key Management Facility East in Culpeper, Virginia. Ceremony 53-1 began at 17:00 UTC on April 25; ceremony 53-2 began at 14:00 UTC on April 26. These cryptographic ceremonies are publicly observable and maintain the trust anchor for global DNSSEC validation.
