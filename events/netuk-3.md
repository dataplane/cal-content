NetUK 3 was held at The Brewery in London, bringing together network architects, engineers, and operators from the UK's internet infrastructure community.

The two-day event opened with morning workshops and newcomer introductions, followed by afternoon technical sessions and an evening social. Day two continued with further sessions covering operational and infrastructure topics relevant to the UK and broader European internet ecosystem.
