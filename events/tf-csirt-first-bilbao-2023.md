The 2023 Bilbao event combined TF-CSIRT closed coordination meetings (January 30–31) with the open FIRST Regional Symposium plenary and keynotes (February 1) and specialized training tracks (February 2). Dataplane.org presented on their sensor network operations. Training covered CSIRT management, malware analysis, DNS security, and cybersecurity simulation exercises.
