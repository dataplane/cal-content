CHI-NOG 10 was held at voco Chicago Downtown (350 West Wolf Point Plaza), running from 8:00 AM through an evening social at 6:00 PM. This was Dataplane.org's first official speaking engagement; Bill Eaheart presented "How NOT to Get Rich Quick: Building an Infrastructure Measurement NFP," sharing lessons from founding and running a nonprofit internet measurement organization.
