DEF CON 31 convened at the Las Vegas Convention Center, bringing together tens of thousands of security researchers, hackers, developers, and enthusiasts. The conference featured talks on vulnerability research, offensive and defensive security techniques, hardware hacking, and policy discussions. Villages focused on specialized topics including network security, social engineering, ICS/SCADA, and AI/ML security. The Capture the Flag (CTF) competition drew top security teams from around the world.
