IGF 2023 convened in Kyoto, Japan, bringing together the global multistakeholder Internet governance community. The meeting's theme, "The Internet We Want — Empowering All People," framed discussions across digital rights, AI governance, cybersecurity, digital inclusion, and the sustainability of Internet infrastructure.

Sessions addressed emerging AI regulation, data governance frameworks, platform accountability, universal connectivity, and the role of the IGF in shaping global digital policy. The Kyoto meeting was notable for its focus on AI and its implications for Internet governance, reflecting the rapid emergence of generative AI as a central policy concern.
