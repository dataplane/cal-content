IGF 2023 met in Kyoto, Japan. The global multistakeholder Internet governance community discussed digital rights, AI governance, cybersecurity, digital inclusion, and Internet infrastructure sustainability under the theme "The Internet We Want — Empowering All People."

Sessions covered emerging AI regulation, data governance frameworks, platform accountability, universal connectivity, and the role of the IGF in shaping global digital policy. AI and its implications for Internet governance drew particular attention, reflecting the rapid rise of generative AI as a policy concern.
