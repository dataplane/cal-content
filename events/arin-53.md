ARIN 53 took place at the Hilton Barbados Resort in Bridgetown, with formal policy sessions running April 15–17 under webcast. The meeting covered proposed policy changes to IPv4 and IPv6 number resource management, routing security through RPKI, and organizational matters for the ARIN service region.
