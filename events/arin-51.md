ARIN 51 convened in San Diego, California, for ARIN's biannual public policy and members meeting. Participants discussed draft policies for IP address management, ARIN's RPKI services, and Number Resource Organization matters. The meeting included both open policy discussions and members-only sessions covering organizational governance and resource stewardship.
