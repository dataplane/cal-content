IGF 2022 met in Addis Ababa, Ethiopia under the auspices of the United Nations, the first time the IGF was held on the African continent. Government representatives, civil society, the private sector, and the technical community attended to discuss Internet governance issues.

Discussions covered digital inclusion, cybersecurity, emerging technologies, data governance, environmental sustainability of the Internet, and the future of Internet governance mechanisms. The forum adopted several messages and outputs; the IGF's mandate renewal was also on the agenda.
