IGF 2022 convened in Addis Ababa, Ethiopia under the auspices of the United Nations, marking the first time the IGF was held on the African continent. The meeting brought together government representatives, civil society, the private sector, and technical community stakeholders to discuss Internet governance issues.

Sessions addressed digital inclusion, cybersecurity, emerging technologies, data governance, environmental sustainability of the Internet, and the future of Internet governance mechanisms. The forum adopted several messages and outputs, and discussions on the future of the IGF mandate were prominent given that the event occurred in the IGF's mandate renewal period.
