Pass the SALT is a community-run conference at the intersection of free/open-source software and security research.

The 2024 edition featured 21 talks spanning WebPKI, DFIR & Threat Intelligence, and Reverse Engineering, plus 9 hands-on workshops. All talks and materials are made freely available after the event.
