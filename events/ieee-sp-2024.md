IEEE S&P 2024 brought together security researchers and practitioners at the Hilton San Francisco Union Square for the 45th edition of this flagship conference. The main symposium ran May 20–22 with workshops on May 23, covering cryptography, network security, privacy-enhancing technologies, and vulnerability research.
