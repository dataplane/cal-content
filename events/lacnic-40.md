LACNIC 40 convened in Cancún, Mexico for LACNIC's biannual meeting covering policy, operations, and Internet development across Latin America and the Caribbean. The meeting included open policy discussions, RPKI and routing security sessions, IPv6 deployment updates, and the LACNIC member assembly.
