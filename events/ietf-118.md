IETF 118 convened at the Hilton Prague hotel in the Czech Republic. Sessions across all IETF working groups addressed routing security (RPKI, ASPA), DNS extensions, transport protocol evolution, post-quantum cryptography, and application-layer standards. Prague is a recurring IETF host city, and the meeting drew strong European participation alongside the global IETF community.
