IETF 118 took place at the Hilton Prague hotel in the Czech Republic. Working groups covered routing security (RPKI, ASPA), DNS extensions, transport protocol evolution, post-quantum cryptography, and application-layer standards. The meeting drew strong European participation alongside the global IETF community.
