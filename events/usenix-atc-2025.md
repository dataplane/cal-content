USENIX ATC '25 was the last ever edition of the Annual Technical Conference, a long-running venue for systems research spanning operating systems, distributed systems, storage, networking, and security. USENIX announced the discontinuation of the ATC series, making this the final edition of a conference that helped shape modern systems software.
