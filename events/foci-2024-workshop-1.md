FOCI (Free and Open Communications on the Internet) 2024 Workshop 1 was held online via Gather.town, running 16:00–22:00 UTC. The workshop covers academic and applied research on internet censorship measurement, circumvention tools, geopolitical blocking, and the technical underpinnings of internet freedom.
