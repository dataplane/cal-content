PacNOG 37 met at the University of South Pacific campus in Suva, Fiji, continuing the organization's capacity development program for network operators across the Pacific Islands.

A Monday plenary conference was followed by three concurrent training tracks running through Friday: DNS and DNSSEC operational practices, network monitoring and management (train-the-trainer), and a practical cybersecurity workshop.
