LKNOG 8 combined a single main conference day at Galle Face Hotel with four days of technical workshops at Renuka City Hotel.

The conference covered cybersecurity, 5G deployment, BGP security, and AI in networking. Workshop tracks focused on Network Security, DNS/DNSSEC, and Network Monitoring.
