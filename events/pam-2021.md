PAM 2021 was the 22nd edition of the International Conference on Passive and Active Measurement. Originally planned for Cottbus, Germany, it moved fully online due to the COVID-19 pandemic. The conference covers network measurement research including topology, traffic, security, and internet infrastructure. Dataplane.org-related research was among the presentations.
