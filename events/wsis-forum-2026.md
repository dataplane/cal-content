WSIS Forum 2026 convened governments, private sector representatives, civil society, academia, and youth for global digital cooperation dialogue, running July 6-7 at ITU Headquarters and July 8-10 at Palexpo.

As the first WSIS Forum following the WSIS+20 UN General Assembly review, the event featured an expanded High Level Track, strengthened ministerial participation, the WSIS Prizes 2026 awards, thematic exhibitions, and specialized tracks for women, girls, and youth, supporting member states in implementing commitments from the December WSIS+20 resolution.
