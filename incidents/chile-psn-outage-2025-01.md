A regional power grid failure in Chile in early 2025 disrupted infrastructure critical to PlayStation Network operations, causing a cascading global outage that lasted approximately 24 hours. The incident affected an estimated 116 million PSN subscribers worldwide who were unable to access online gaming, digital purchases, or streaming services.
