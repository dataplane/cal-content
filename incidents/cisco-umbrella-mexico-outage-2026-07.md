Cisco Umbrella experienced DNS and Secure Web Gateway timeout errors beginning July 10, 2026 at 23:21 UTC, caused by a fiber cut affecting its MEX1 EDC site in the Mexico region. Customers saw intermittent DNS resolution failures, 504/50X timeout errors, and, in some cases, unintended geo-blocking on location-sensitive sites.

Cisco's operations team temporarily rerouted traffic away from the affected site to limit disruption while repairs were underway. The fiber cut was resolved and the data center restored to normal operation by July 11 at 10:20 UTC.
