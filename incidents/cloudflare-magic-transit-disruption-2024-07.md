Cloudflare's Magic Transit product, which provides network-layer DDoS protection for enterprise customers by announcing their IP prefixes through Cloudflare's anycast network for scrubbing, experienced a service disruption on July 9, 2024. Affected enterprise customers temporarily lost DDoS mitigation coverage for their prefix blocks while the incident was active.
