Gcore's network services in Atlanta went fully unavailable starting July 8, 2026 at 1:54pm GMT, with partial CDN performance degradation extending across North America. Gcore did not disclose a root cause.

Engineers rerouted traffic away from Atlanta while investigating, and restored normal operations by July 9 at 3:53pm GMT — roughly a day after the outage began.
