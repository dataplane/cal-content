OVHcloud's GRA datacenter experienced a network service disruption beginning July 8, 2026 at 14:50 UTC, attributed to an unexpected infrastructure malfunction in the Network Core. Customers reported possible disruption, latency, or packet loss on network services during the incident window.

The disruption ran unusually long, with OVHcloud confirming full resolution and completed restoration efforts on July 13, 2026 at 11:10 UTC — nearly five days after it began.
