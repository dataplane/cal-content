The University of Oregon's RouteViews project, which collects BGP routing table data from dozens of participating networks around the world, experienced service degradation in mid-August 2024. RouteViews is a foundational resource for internet routing research, providing free access to live BGP feeds and historical routing table snapshots. The degradation was tracked by BGPKit's status monitoring system.
