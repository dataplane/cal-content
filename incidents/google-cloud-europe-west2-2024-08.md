On August 12, 2024, Google Cloud's europe-west2 availability zone (London) experienced a service disruption that cascaded beyond regional infrastructure to impact globally-served Google products. Users worldwide experienced degraded or unavailable access to Google Search, Gmail, and YouTube.

ThousandEyes published technical analysis of the incident.
