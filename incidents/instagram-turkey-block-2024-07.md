Turkish authorities blocked access to Instagram in early August 2024. The block was reportedly triggered by Instagram hosting condolence messages related to the assassination of Hamas political leader Ismail Haniyeh, which Turkish officials viewed as inappropriate content on the platform. The block was documented by NetBlocks and confirmed through OONI measurements, affecting tens of millions of Instagram users in Turkey.
