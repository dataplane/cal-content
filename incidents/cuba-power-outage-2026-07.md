Cuba experienced another nation-wide power blackout ("apagón") on July 6, 2026, amid the island's ongoing fuel shortages. NetBlocks confirmed a significant decline in internet traffic corresponding to the outage.

Cloudflare Radar and IODA active-probing data both showed traffic drops beginning around 16:00 UTC, consistent with the timing of the blackout. The event follows a recurring pattern of power-driven connectivity loss in Cuba, where an aging and fuel-constrained electrical grid has made internet availability closely tied to the state of the power supply.
