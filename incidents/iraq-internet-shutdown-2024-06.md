Iraq implemented a deliberate, government-mandated internet shutdown on June 24, 2024 during the national examination period. This has been a recurring practice in Iraq in recent years, with authorities cutting mobile internet access nationwide during exam hours to prevent cheating. The shutdown was documented by NetBlocks, IODA, and Cloudflare Radar.
