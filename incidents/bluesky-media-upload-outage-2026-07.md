Bluesky reported an outage on July 9, 2026, caused by an issue with one of its cloud hosting providers. Images and videos failed to render and upload, and some AT Protocol services — including Jetstream, the firehose used by third-party clients and bots — experienced disruption.

Bluesky's status account marked the incident resolved shortly after posting, but users in multiple regions, including the UK and Australia, continued reporting video upload failures for days afterward.
