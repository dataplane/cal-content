Hurricane Ernesto crossed through the Caribbean in mid-August 2024, bringing heavy rainfall and high winds that disrupted power and communications infrastructure. Internet connectivity impacts were observed across affected islands including Puerto Rico, tracked in real-time by Cloudflare Radar and NetBlocks. The storm later struck Bermuda as a Category 2 hurricane before transitioning to post-tropical status over the North Atlantic.
